Privacy policy

hermess is used only by its owner, to read the owner's own Google account data (mail, calendar, tasks and files).

  • Data is processed on the owner's own server.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.
  • No data about other people is collected, and nobody else can sign in.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
